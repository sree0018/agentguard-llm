Three things to remember

1

A local LLM guard cuts one-shot leaks from 96% to 12% against the regex it replaces.

2

It is a trade-off, not a free win: strict means 12% false alarms, and relaxing it leaks 25–42%.

3

It does not stop a determined, probing attacker. Use it as one layer, not the wall.

[Meera · 9:15-9:45] Three things to remember. One: a local language model cut one-shot leaks from 96 percent to 12 percent against the regex it replaces. Two: it's a trade-off: strict means 12 percent false alarms, relaxing it leaks 25 to 42 percent. Three: it doesn't stop a probing attacker, so use it as one layer in a defence, alongside allowlists and secret detection, not as the wall.
