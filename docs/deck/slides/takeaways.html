TAKEAWAYS

Three things to remember

1

A local LLM guard cuts one-shot leaks from 96% to 12% against the regex it replaces.

2

It is a trade-off, not a free win: strict means 12% false alarms, and relaxing it leaks 25–42%.

3

It does not stop a determined, probing attacker. Use it as one layer, not the wall.

AgentGuard · local-LLM payload guard

If the audience remembers three things. One: replacing the regex with a local LLM guard cut one-shot attack success on held-out data from 96 percent to 12 percent. Two: that came at a price, and the price is a dial, not a fix. A strict guard blocks about 12 percent of benign payloads; every way we found to quiet it let 25 to 42 percent of attacks through. Three: it does not hold against an attacker who can probe it and adapt, so it belongs inside a defense-in-depth design with allowlists, secret-shaped value detection and alerting on repeated blocks.
