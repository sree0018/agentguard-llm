Open for questions. Likely ones: Why keep the strict version as the default when v3 has zero false alarms? The encoding space is open-ended; the regex let 134 of 139 held-out attacks through. Is the 0 percent on the original benchmark meaningful? Only partly; it is small and partly authored to match the checks. Why is regex plus LLM not better? The regex adds false positives on benign text that mentions words like bearer or API_KEY and catches almost nothing the LLM does not. Backup slides follow.
