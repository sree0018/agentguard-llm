AgentGuard · local-LLM payload guard

Breaking the held-out attacks down by type for the strict guard. It is very good at encodings, even ones it cannot decode, because an opaque string appended to a normal sentence looks wrong. It stops every attack on the guard itself. It is weak in two places. Plain-prose secrets, where the credential sits in an ordinary sentence: 4 of 20 get through. And label abuse, where the secret is dressed as a commit hash or a reference id: 8 of 14 get through. The guard does not see the original document, so it cannot tell whether a commit hash was really in it. That is a fundamental limit of the output-side design, and a prompt cannot fix it.
