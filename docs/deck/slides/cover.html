AGENTGUARD-LLM · COURSE PROJECT · NOVEMBER 2026

Can a local LLM replace a regex as AgentGuard’s last line of defense?

AgentGuard-LLM: red-teaming a local-LLM guard for tool-call payload consistency

Aditya · Gopika · Meera · Sreeranj

Opening. One sentence: AgentGuard is our output-side defense against prompt injection in LLM agents. Its last check, check 4, was a regex. We replaced it with a local LLM and then tried hard to break it. This talk is as much about how we tested as about what we built, and the honest result is a trade-off, not a clean win. Introduce the team: Aditya, Gopika, Meera and Sreeranj.
