AgentGuard · local-LLM payload guard

[BACKUP, not in the 10 minutes · questions go to Sreeranj] The price of using a language model. The regex costs about two microseconds. The LLM guard costs about four seconds per call at the median and six and a half at the 95th percentile on an M-series laptop with llama3.1:8b through Ollama; the model is about five gigabytes. Two mitigations: the guard only runs when checks 1 to 3 pass, and it can be restricted to tools that actually carry free-text payloads. Whether four seconds is acceptable depends on the application. It is fine for an assistant that sends an email or posts a comment, and not fine for something that makes hundreds of calls a minute. Latency figures come from the cached original calls, so they are replayable.
