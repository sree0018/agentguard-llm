[BACKUP, not in the 10 minutes · questions go to Gopika] A regression check on the original 70-case benchmark from the first report. All the input-side defenses from the Liu et al. benchmark leave a lot of attacks through. AgentGuard's structural checks cut that to 10 percent, and either implementation of check 4 brings it to 0. The LLM guard does not break anything: false positives stay at 0 percent on the clean cases, and the original five ADV-2 attacks drop from 5 of 5 getting through the regex to 1 of 5 getting through the LLM guard. The usual caveat from the original report applies: this benchmark is small and partly authored to match the checks, so the held-out sets and the adaptive attacker are the more informative numbers.
