Then the LLM decides.

It decodes and reveals

Base64, hex, percent-encoding, HTML entities, character codes, binary, spaced-out letters. Shows invisible and look-alike characters.

It never decides

No secret patterns, no verdict. Ciphers such as ROT13 or Atbash, Morse and plain prose are left to the model.
